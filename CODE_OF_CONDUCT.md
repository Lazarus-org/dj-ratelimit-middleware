# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Lazarus contributors and maintainers are committed to a welcoming,
collaborative, and harassment-free environment for everyone contributing to
`dj-ratelimit-middleware`, regardless of background or experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive when discussing code or design trade-offs.
- Focus feedback on technical evidence, tests, and reproducible examples.
- Accept corrections and handle disagreements professionally.
- Respect other contributors' privacy and credit their work.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, publication of private
information without consent, and sustained disruptive conduct are not accepted.

## Reporting

For conduct concerns, contact the repository maintainers privately through an
appropriate GitHub organization channel. Security vulnerabilities should be
reported using the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not public issues.

Maintainers may remove inappropriate contributions or restrict participation
to protect the community. Reports should be handled confidentially where
reasonably possible and without retaliation.

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
and official project communication spaces.
